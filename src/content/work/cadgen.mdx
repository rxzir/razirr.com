Amidst the Covid-19 pandemic, physical stores faced prolonged closures, prompting a surge in online grocery shopping—a trend that persisted even post-lockdown. This transition dealt a significant blow to small businesses lacking an online presence.

The campaign launched hyper-targeted digital ads during Diwali, achieving notable first-year results: 1,800 stores, 270 pincodes, 8 cities, 32% growth, 20m+ users, 2x sales.

DeltaX was the brains behind the operation. For the second year, we took it even further. Users could generate personalized videos, or store owners could create content featuring Shah Rukh Khan.
