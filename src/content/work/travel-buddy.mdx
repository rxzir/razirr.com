A warm and helpful travel companion mobile app that not only assists in exploration and planning of trips but also guides you seamlessly throughout your journey.

## Not just another travel app…

The goal was to merge the structured nature of travel packages with the flexibility of personalised planning, creating an all-encompassing travel companion, with community itinerary sharing via the 'Explore' feature.

## What stands out…

Key differentiators include remarkable adaptability, allowing users to modify AI generated trip plans, and the option to select plans crafted by fellow travellers.

## Jumping into Figma…

The information architecture and user flow serve as the blueprint for our application, providing a solid foundation for screen design, incorporating insights from various travel apps.
