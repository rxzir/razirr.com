## Not just another travel app…

Our goal was to merge the structured nature of travel packages with the flexibility of personalised planning, creating an all-encompassing travel companion. But it doesn't stop there – with 'Explore', you can follow along a fellow travellers' itineraries or showcase your perfect plan for the world to see.

<Image src={travelBuddyNotJustAnotherApp} alt="Travel Buddy — Not just another travel app" />

## What stands out…

The standout features of the application lie in its remarkable adaptability, allowing users to modify AI generated trip plans, and the option to select plans crafted by fellow travellers. These functionalities add a unique layer of customisation and community-driven travel experiences.

<Image src={travelBuddyWhatStandsOut} alt="Travel Buddy — What stands out" />

## Jumping into Figma…

The information architecture and user flow serve as the blueprint for our application, providing a solid foundation for screen design. Drawing insights from various travel apps, I combined their standout features to craft an intuitive layout for seamless plan creation and exploration.

## Meanwhile, in the background…

Understanding the needs and experiences of our target users paved the way for crafting a purposeful application. We carefully considered their pain points in order to shape the structure and features of the app, ensuring a seamless and user-centric experience.

The designs were based on deep insights of user needs gathered when asked about their ultimate travel app wishlist.
